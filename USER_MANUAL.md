## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Creating an Account
To start using Mistribhai, you need an account.
1. Click **Create one free** on the homepage.
2. Fill in your Name, Email, and Password.
3. Choose your Role:
   - **Finder**: If you want to post jobs and hire professionals.
   - **Provider**: If you want to offer your services and get hired.
4. Click **Register**. You will receive an email verification link (in development mode, the link is printed in the server console). Click it to verify your account.

### 2. Logging In
1. Go to the Sign In page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**. You will be redirected to your specific dashboard based on your role.

## 🔍 Using Mistribhai as a Finder

As a Finder, you can post jobs, get AI-matched with the best Providers, review their applications, and hire them.

### 1. The Finder Dashboard
Your dashboard gives you a quick overview of your activity:
- **Active Posts:** Number of open jobs you have posted.
- **Total Matches:** Total number of providers matched to your posts.
- **Messages:** Unread messages from providers.

### 2. Posting a Job (Service Post)
1. Click **Post a Job** on your dashboard or navigation bar.
2. Fill out the details:
   - **Title**: E.g., "Need a web developer for e-commerce site".
   - **Description**: Provide as much detail as possible.
   - **Budget**: Set your expected budget for the job.
   - **Location**: Provide the location or state it's "Remote".
3. Click **Post Job**. Our Gemini AI will immediately analyze your post and find the best-matching Providers.

### 3. Reviewing AI Matches & Applications
1. Go to your dashboard and click **View Matches** on one of your active posts.
2. You will see a list of Providers that match your job requirements, ranked by an AI-generated match score.
3. You can click on a Provider to view their **Public Profile** (portfolio, experience, skills, and past reviews).
4. **Applications:** Providers can apply to your job. You can view applications and choose to **Accept** or **Reject** them.

### 4. Leaving a Review
Once a job is marked as "Completed," you can leave a review for the Provider:
1. Navigate to the job post.
2. Click **Leave a Review** next to the hired Provider.
3. Give a 1-5 star rating and leave a text review.

## 💼 Using Mistribhai as a Provider

As a Provider, you can showcase your skills, get matched to relevant jobs, apply to jobs, and build a reputation.

### 1. Completing Your Profile
To get better AI matches, you need a complete profile.
1. Go to your **Profile**.
2. Upload a **Profile Image** and **Cover Image**.
3. Fill out your **Professional Title**, **Bio/Description**, **Hourly Rate**, and **Experience**.
4. Upload **Portfolio Images** to showcase your past work.

### 2. Adding Skills
Our AI uses your skills to match you to jobs.
1. On your **Dashboard**, go to the **My Skills** section.
2. Add relevant skills and choose your proficiency level (Beginner, Intermediate, Expert).
3. The more detailed your skills, the better your matches!

### 3. Finding & Applying to Jobs
1. Go to your Dashboard and click **View Best Matches**.
2. You will see a list of job posts that match your skill set and profile description.
3. Click on a job to view its details.
4. If it's a good fit, click **Apply to Job**. 
5. The Finder will review your application and can choose to Accept it.

### 4. Job Status Workflow
When a Finder accepts your application, the job transitions from **Open** to **In Progress**. Once you finish the work, the Finder can mark it as **Completed** and leave you a review, boosting your public rating.

## 💬 In-App Messaging

Once a Provider has applied to a job, or a Finder wants to contact a matched Provider, they can use the messaging system.
1. Click the **Messages** icon in the navigation bar.
2. Select a conversation to open the chat window.
3. You can send real-time text messages to discuss job details, share links, and negotiate terms before finalizing the job.

## ⚙️ Account Settings

1. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Here you can:
   - Change your Name or Email.
   - Update your Password.
   - Toggle **Email Notifications** on or off.
   - (Providers only) Toggle your **Profile Visibility** (turn off if you are not currently looking for work).
3. **Delete Account:** If you wish to leave the platform, there is an option in the Danger Zone at the bottom of the Settings page to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
