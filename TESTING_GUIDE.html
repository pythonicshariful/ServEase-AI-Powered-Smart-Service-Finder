Mistribhai Testing Guide

A complete walkthrough to test all features of the AI-Powered Smart Service Finder.

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to simulate a complete lifecycle on the Mistribhai platform. You will test the system from three different perspectives: the Finder, the Provider, and the Headquarters (Admin).

1. The Service Finder (Client)
Demo Credentials:

Email: finder@example.com

Password: 1234

What to test:
  • Log In: Go to the Sign In page and log in with the Finder credentials.
  • Update Profile: Navigate to the Profile section. Upload a profile picture, add a bio, and fill out company details.
  • Post a Job: Click Post a Job (or the plus icon on mobile). Fill out a title, detailed description, location, and budget. Submit the job.
  • Check AI Matches: After posting, click View Matches on your job. You should see a list of Providers ranked by the Gemini AI based on how well their skills match your job description.
  • Message a Provider: Click on a matched Provider's profile and click Message to initiate a chat.
  • Review Applications: When a Provider applies to your job, click View Applications on your dashboard and Accept their application.
  • Leave a Review: Once a job is accepted and finished, mark it as completed and leave a 1-5 star review for the Provider.
2. The Service Provider (Professional)
Demo Credentials:

Email: prov@example.com

Password: 1234

What to test:
  • Log In: Log out of the Finder account and log in with the Provider credentials.
  • Build Your Portfolio: Go to Profile. Add your Business Name, Hourly Rate, and upload Portfolio Images so clients can see your work.
  • Add Skills: On your Dashboard, find the My Skills section. Add specific skills (e.g., "Plumbing", "Electrical") and set your proficiency level. The AI uses these to match you!
  • Find Jobs: Click Matches in the navigation bar. You will see open jobs that the AI thinks you are a good fit for.
  • Apply to a Job: Click on the job the Finder just posted, and click Apply to Job.
  • Respond to Messages: Go to your Inbox to respond to any real-time messages sent by the Finder.
  • Check Reviews: Once the Finder leaves a review, go to your Public Profile to see your new rating and review comments.
3. System Headquarters (Admin)
Demo Credentials:

Email: prov@example.com

Password: 1234

(Note: We have elevated the demo provider account to also have Admin privileges for testing purposes.)
What to test:
  • Access Headquarters: Log in as prov@example.com. In the top navigation bar, click the purple Admin Headquarters button.
  • Dashboard Metrics: View the real-time statistics for Total Users, Active Posts, and Messages.
  • Maintenance Mode: Click the Turn Maintenance Mode ON button. Open a private/incognito window (so you are not logged in as an admin) and try to visit the site. You should see the "We'll be right back" page. Turn it back off when done.
  • User Management: Go to the Users tab. Try suspending a test user (create a dummy account first if needed). Suspended users will not be able to log in.
  • Provider Verification: Go to the Providers tab. Click Verify on a provider. When you view their public profile later, they will have a green "Verified" checkmark.
  • Content Moderation: Go to the Moderation tab. Find the job post or review you created earlier and click Hide. Verify it disappears from public view.
  • Global Broadcasts: Go to the Broadcasts tab. Create a new announcement (e.g., "Welcome to Mistribhai!"). This is permanently logged in the system announcements database.

Mistribhai is built with Flask, Bootstrap, and powered by Google Gemini AI.
